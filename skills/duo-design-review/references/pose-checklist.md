# Pose checklist

Answer each question with yes, no or cannot tell. "Cannot tell" from a still image is a valid answer and goes to the verification list.

## 1. Closed, portrait (outer display, compact width)

- Is this a complete compact-width layout, not a cropped one?
- Are navigation, toolbar and tab items in the side bar, in the order back or close, then the prominent action, then the rest?
- Does every bar item have a symbol? Text-only items stay horizontal and cost height.
- Is content inset from the bar on that side only, with backgrounds running to the edge?
- Is the corner with the camera free of content and controls?
- Do the actions that matter most stay visible when the bar overflows? Is the screen's one main action named, so it can be pinned?
- Where a toolbar and tabs share the bar, is it stated which gives way first: tabs kept on navigation screens, actions kept on task screens?

## 2. Closed, landscape or standing like a tent (compact width, compact height)

- Does the layout work with very little height? What scrolls?
- Which items go to the overflow menu, and are the important ones kept? This pose has the least room: expect little beyond the main action to stay.
- Does every action that can overflow have a title that reads well in a menu, without its symbol?
- Is the screen useful seen from a distance if the app has a hands-free use?

## 3. Open, landscape (inner display, regular width)

- Is the width used for more of the hierarchy: a split view, a second column, a sidebar? Or is it the phone layout stretched?
- For content screens: are there two pages either side of the fold? If the screen spans instead, is the reason stated?
- Are the same functions present as in pose 1?
- Are bar items in the same order and relative position as in pose 1?
- Do grids have an even number of columns?
- Does each page fill its own side of the fold? The page beside the bar is narrower; an empty band at the outer edge of the other page is wasted space unless equal pages have a stated reason.
- Does the design still work with the narrow page on the other side, as in a right-to-left language?

## 4. Partially folded, like a book (fold runs top to bottom)

- Does any control, line of text, face or continuous image cross the fold?
- Compared with pose 3, did anything move more than it had to?
- Did transient elements — alerts, sheets, popovers — land on one side, preferably the trailing one?
- Are both sides usable on their own?

## 5. Open, portrait (regular width, tall)

- Does the layout work with horizontal bars at the top and bottom?
- Is the tall canvas used, or is there dead space?
- Is anything lost compared with landscape?

## 6. Partially folded, like a laptop (fold runs side to side)

- Is what people look at above the fold and what they touch below it?
- Is anything interactive in the fold?
- Do media controls, keyboards or toolbars sit on the lower half?
- If the layout does not change in this pose, is that acceptable because the content scrolls?

## 7. Split View, either half (compact width)

- Does the compact layout appear, the same one as on the outer display?
- With the app on the left, the bar is on its left edge; on the right, on its right edge. Does content stay clear of the bar in both?
- Is anything that needs width, such as a second column, reachable another way?

## 8. Transitions

- Closing while reading or editing: is the same item shown on the outer display, at the same place?
- Opening: does the item stay selected, with the added column showing its context?
- Is unsaved input kept?
- Does a sheet, alert or menu that is open survive the change?
- If the app offers a second window, is that offered only where it can work?
